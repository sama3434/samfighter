Mode screen — VS PLAYER, or VS COMPUTER at five difficulty levels; either player's keys drive it

Select screen — your own movement keys: left/right to choose, up to lock in, down to cancel. Against the computer you use Player 1's keys; the computer picks its own fighter. Esc returns to the mode screen

Crouch + kick = sweep (knockdown)

Jump + punch/kick = air attack

Special — 4 kicks or 8 punches fills the bar; the spin hits both sides and stuns for 0.7s

First to 2 rounds wins  •  Enter restarts or returns to the roster
